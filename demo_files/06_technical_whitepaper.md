## 1. Abstract
LumenPipe is a high-bandwidth optical air-gapped data transport protocol designed to bridge isolated computer networks.
By encoding arbitrary binary files into high-density animated QR codes modulated with Luby Transform (LT) fountain codes,
it establishes a strictly unidirectional, simplex transmission medium that eliminates all radio-frequency vulnerabilities.

## 2. Threat Model & Physical Air-Gap Isolation
Conventional file-transfer methods (USB drives, Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, NFC) present severe attack vectors:
- BadUSB and microcontroller firmware reprogramming.
- Two-way RF exploitation and side-channel eavesdropping.
- Covert backchannel beaconing and malware command-and-control.

LumenPipe solves this by using visible light photons emitted from a monitor to an untethered camera.
The photon path is physically incapable of transmitting data backwards.

## 3. Mathematical Foundations of LT Fountain Codes
Given a file of size S deflated with zlib, we split the data into K equal source chunks:
$$\mathcal{C} = \{c_0, c_1, \dots, c_{K-1}\}, \quad c_i \in \mathbb{F}_2^B$$

For every animated droplet tick:
1. A 32-bit PRNG seed is generated via Mulberry32.
2. A degree d is sampled from the Robust Soliton distribution $\Omega(d)$:
$$\mu(i) = \begin{cases} 1/K & i = 1 \\ 1/(i(i-1)) & 2 \le i \le K \end{cases}$$
3. d distinct indices are selected, and their chunk contents are XORed in $\mathbb{F}_2$.
4. The receiver maintains a lower-triangular pivot matrix and solves the system via incremental Gaussian elimination.
